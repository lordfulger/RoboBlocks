Getting started

  • Press Play or Quick play. The Tutorial on the main menu walks you through building and driving.
  • Make an account (Guest · Sign in on the menu card) so your level and garage are saved on this server.
  • Drive with W A S D, aim with the mouse, click to fire, Shift to boost. Phones and tablets get touch controls.
  • In the garage press ? for every shortcut.

Found a problem?

  • Press F8 anywhere in the game (or Report a bug in the pause menu) and tell us what happened. The game adds the technical details for you.
  • Press F3 to see frame rate and ping if things feel slow, and include them in your report.
  • To report a player, open the pause menu during a match, or type /report name reason in chat.

Your progress and garage are stored on this server. Bug reports include your browser, the match you were in and recent error messages, nothing else.
